El Pollo Loco

Tutorial

Use the arrow keys to move right and left, and space to jump. Collect bottles and press 'D' to throw them on enimies. You can also jump on chickens to kill them, but be careful not to get hit by them! Collect coins from killing chickens to increase your score. Endboss will spawn after killing all chickens. Defeat the Endboss to win the game!
