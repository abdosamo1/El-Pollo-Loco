El Pollo Loco

You must kill all chickens to spawn the Endboss if you want to win

How to Play

Movement
Use the arrow keys (◀ ▶) or the on-screen buttons to walk left and right. Press Space, Arrow Up, or the ▲ button to jump.

Defeating Chickens
Jump on top of a regular or small chicken to squash it instantly. Landing on it while it's still on the ground (not mid-jump) won't work - you have to actually be airborne and falling onto it.

Bottles
Collect the salsa bottles scattered across the level (bottle bar in the top-left). Press 'D' or the 🧴 button to throw one at an enemy - this also instantly defeats regular and small chickens. If you run out, new bottles spawn ahead of you every few seconds.

Taking Damage
Touching a chicken without stomping it (or getting hit by the Endboss) costs health, knocks Pepe back, and briefly stops him from moving. Watch your health bar - if it runs out, it's game over.

Coins & Score
Defeated chickens sometimes drop coins. Collect them to fill your coin bar - your final coin percentage is your score when you win.

The Endboss
Once every regular chicken is defeated, the Endboss appears. He can't be stomped - only thrown bottles can hurt him. Defeat him to win the game!

Other Controls
Use the pause button to pause/resume, the speaker button to mute/unmute all sounds, and the fullscreen button to toggle fullscreen.
